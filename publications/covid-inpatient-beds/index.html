Tracking COVID-19 Inpatient Bed Occupancy

How many inpatient beds were occupied by COVID-19 patients, and what share of hospital capacity did that represent? For our San Jose State University biomedical engineering term project, we examined daily estimates from January 14 through February 13, 2021.

Tanwani Tanwani and Austin Wong · BME 133 · 2023

What did a typical report look like?

The median estimate was 996.5 occupied beds per record, compared with a mean of 3,921. Large counts pull the mean upward. Looking at capacity instead of counts, the average reported occupancy was 11.56%. That is an unweighted average of reporting percentages, not the percentage of all U.S. beds occupied.

From CSV to dashboard

Our Python analyzer used pandas to parse dates, convert comma-formatted counts to numbers, and summarize the estimates. We plotted daily means with Matplotlib and built a local Dash app with a Plotly line chart of daily sums. The file includes a countrywide reporting code alongside state-level records, so adding every row can double-count beds.
